Why Tysons Homeowners Invest in Exterior Upgrades

Choose Materials That Suit the Property

Every exterior project has different requirements. Asphalt, concrete, pavers, brick, bluestone, natural stone, and other materials each offer different appearances and performance characteristics. We help you select an approach that fits the property and intended use.

Add Lasting Value to Outdoor Spaces

Home Improvement Services for Tysons Exteriors

Our exterior services cover the surfaces and masonry features that carry the most use around a property, from the driveway at the street to the patio at the back of the house.

Driveway Paving

We install and replace asphalt, concrete, and paver driveways for Tysons properties. Proper excavation, base preparation, grading, and material installation help create a surface designed for regular vehicle use.

Brick Construction & Repairs

Our brickwork includes patios, entryways, steps, facades, and repointing. We can work with existing brickwork to maintain a cohesive appearance while addressing areas that need repair or replacement.

Retaining Wall Construction

Retaining walls can help manage changes in elevation, control soil movement, and reduce erosion. We install natural stone and segmental block walls based on the site's layout and structural requirements.

Foundation Repairs

Cracks and other foundation concerns should receive attention before they become more extensive. Our exterior foundation repair work includes crack repair, stabilization, and restoration based on the condition of the affected area.

Patio Installation & Design

Create a more functional outdoor gathering area with a patio designed around the available space. We work with pavers, natural stone, bluestone, brick, and stamped concrete for patio installations.

Walkways & Steps

Stonework & Veneer

Outdoor Fireplaces & Fire Pits

An outdoor fireplace or fire pit can give a patio or entertaining area a clear focal point. We build custom exterior features that can incorporate seating walls, storage, and other practical elements where appropriate.
