Create Consistent Exterior Features

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What exterior projects can Onyx Home Improvement complete in McLean?

Onyx works on a range of residential exterior projects, including driveways, patios, walkways, paving, masonry, brickwork, stonework, retaining walls, asphalt, concrete, and exterior repairs.

2. Can Onyx help replace an old or damaged driveway?

Yes. Driveway projects can be planned around the property's available space, existing conditions, drainage, vehicle use, and the type of surface required.

3. How do I know which material is suitable for my exterior project?

The appropriate material depends on the application, existing property, site conditions, intended use, and desired appearance. Onyx can consider these factors when planning the exterior work.

4. Can a new patio be connected to an existing walkway or driveway?

Yes. Multiple outdoor features can be planned together so that their positioning, materials, and overall layout work cohesively with the existing property.

5. What exterior areas can benefit from masonry work?

Masonry can be used across various outdoor features, including walls, entrances, steps, patios, and other areas where structural or decorative exterior construction is needed.

6. Does exterior remodeling require consideration of drainage?

Yes. Water management is an important consideration for many exterior projects. Site grading, drainage conditions, and the position of paved or masonry features should be evaluated during project planning.

7. Can Onyx repair existing exterior masonry?

Yes. Exterior repair work can address areas of damaged or deteriorating masonry. The appropriate approach depends on the condition and type of the existing feature.

8. Why choose an exterior-focused contractor for my McLean property?

An exterior-focused contractor understands the specific considerations involved with outdoor construction, including paving, drainage, weather exposure, material selection, site preparation, and integration with existing landscape features.
