Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does Onyx Home Improvement provide interior remodeling services?

Onyx Home Improvement focuses on exterior projects, including masonry, brickwork, stonework, paving, driveways, patios, walkways, retaining walls, asphalt, concrete, foundation repair, and chimney repair.

2. What exterior projects can you complete for Falls Church homeowners?

Services include masonry and brickwork, driveway paving, retaining walls, stone veneer, patio design, outdoor fireplaces, walkways, and exterior structural repairs. Projects can be tailored to the property's existing features and layout.

3. What should I consider when planning home remodeling in Falls Church, VA?

Start by identifying the exterior areas that need improvement and consider how the project will affect access, drainage, appearance, and outdoor use. Material selection and compatibility with existing features are also important.

4. Can exterior remodeling improve the appearance of an older property?

Yes. Updated paving, masonry, stonework, brick features, patios, and walkways can refresh the exterior while maintaining the property's existing character. The design can be coordinated with the home's architecture and landscape.

5. Do you provide asphalt and concrete work?

Yes. Asphalt and concrete can be used for exterior applications such as driveways, walkways, and other hardscape areas where a durable surface is required.

6. What makes a good remodeling contractor in Falls Church, VA for an exterior project?

Look for experience with the specific type of exterior work you need, knowledge of appropriate materials, careful preparation, and clear communication. It is also helpful to choose a contractor who considers how new features will interact with the existing property.

7. Can you combine several exterior improvements into one project?

Multiple exterior features can be planned together to create a more cohesive result. For example, a driveway, walkway, patio, and stonework can be designed to complement one another in materials, layout, and appearance.

8. How can I make my outdoor space more useful?

Consider how you want to use the area and then plan features around those activities. A combination of patios, walkways, retaining walls, stonework, or an outdoor fireplace can give different sections of the property a clear purpose.
